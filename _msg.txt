Compose the head per label space so unseen answers keep the pretrained rows

The previous commit kept the trained shared rows for every label space the reserved
letter rows cover, which meant a question that is *not* in the training mix was answered
by rows tuned for other questions (the demo's "escalate" flipped from yes 0.93 to no 0.61).

scripts/compose_wide_rows.py now builds the shipped head in three parts:
- rows 0..SLOT_RESERVE-1 stay the pretrained label rows (the fallback for an unseen answer)
- every label space in the training mix gets its own block: rows copied from the trained
  head by the option's position in the annotated file (not its alphabetical position -
  FEVER's supports/refutes/not_enough_info order matters), or prototype rows when the
  label space is wider than the reserved budget
- anything else still falls back to the reserved rows

625 rows = 27 reserved + 598 private, covering 90 label spaces. The full benchmark
reproduces the previous numbers exactly (bool 0.794, choice 0.671 over 18 tasks, score
0.737, overall 0.713 against Laya 0.424); one MMLU-Pro item flipped, so its row in the
report is 0.362. Verified with the dev slice (25 tasks identical) and `python demo.py`.
